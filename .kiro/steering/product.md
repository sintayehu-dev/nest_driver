# Product Overview

**Nest Driver** is a Flutter mobile application for driver management and operations. The app serves as a driver-focused platform with features including:

- **Authentication & Profile Management**: OTP-based login, profile updates, and role selection
- **Driver Operations**: Home dashboard, trip history, earnings tracking, and messaging
- **Registration System**: Driver registration and onboarding process
- **Multi-platform Support**: iOS, Android, Web, Windows, macOS, and Linux

The application follows a multi-environment setup (development, staging, production) and is designed for scalability with clean architecture principles.

## Key User Flows
- Driver authentication via OTP
- Profile management and updates
- Trip and earnings tracking
- Real-time messaging and notifications
- Registration and onboarding for new drivers
